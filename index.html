15-year timeline

Three strategic phases

Each phase builds on the last — from establishing a strong foundation, to scaling interdisciplinary research, to becoming a globally recognized AI leadership hub.

I
2026 – 2030
Foundation
Build the infrastructure, talent, and culture needed for sustainable AI research and education. Establish the core identity of COCAI.
Launch AI literacy curriculum for all KKU undergraduates
Establish 3 seed research labs (GeoAI, MedAI, HCAI)
Hire 15+ AI-specialized faculty across domains
First KKU–Hospital AI pilot programs live
Quantum computing curriculum and simulation lab
II
2030 – 2035
Expansion
Scale research centers, deepen industry and hospital partnerships, and launch flagship interdisciplinary degree programs with international reach.
Open 6 domain-specific research centers
Launch MSc and PhD in AI + Domain (GeoAI, MedAI, Quantum)
ASEAN AI Ethics Framework published and adopted
50+ industry and government partnerships active
Thai-language foundation model released open source
III
2035 – 2040
Leadership
Achieve recognition as Southeast Asia's premier AI research and education hub, with deep policy influence, global publications, and impactful spin-offs.
Top 3 AI research institution ranking in ASEAN
10+ deep-tech spin-offs from COCAI research
500+ AI researchers and alumni in the ecosystem
Annual ASEAN AI Summit hosted at KKU
Quantum computing center with national designation
